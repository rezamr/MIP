## RAW reconstructed report

The participant reported perceiving a very beautiful pale/light-blue energy-like field. Deliberate visualization of the protective bubble from the body's own first-person viewpoint was difficult. Instead, the participant experienced or imagined the self from an external viewpoint within a protective bubble.

The bubble was pale/light blue, sometimes becoming more saturated. It was perceived as almost stable but with very slight pulsation and a circulating quality.

A phone notification occurred unexpectedly. At that moment the bubble appeared to shift toward red. The participant internally reassured themself that nothing could harm them and that the protective bubble provided complete protection. The perceived field then returned to its prior normal state.

A yellow energy-like connection was perceived between the ground and the protective bubble. The participant described it as cable-like but made of the same energy-like substance.

Later in the session, the participant reported rotation/drifting and seeing new material. Attention repeatedly wandered. The participant used an internal instruction equivalent to: distraction is irrelevant; focus on the target.

A spontaneous image/scene involving having a very beautiful blonde daughter appeared. The participant noted that this detail was unexpected and did not match their own hair color.

The session was interrupted near the end by a phone call because the phone had not been silenced.

On interruption/return, the participant reported strong numbness/heaviness and a subjective sensation described as if the self had suddenly returned to the body.

The participant's subjective estimate of elapsed time was less than approximately 10 minutes. After checking the phone, substantially more time had passed, reported in conversation as roughly 40–60 minutes. Because this record is retrospective and the exact wording/timestamp is ambiguous, the exact elapsed time is `UNKNOWN`.
