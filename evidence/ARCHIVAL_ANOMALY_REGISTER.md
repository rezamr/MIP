## AA-001 — Missing page 25

- Artifact: *Analysis and Assessment of Gateway Process*
- Date: 9 June 1983
- Organization: US Army / INSCOM context
- Observed anomaly: widely circulated government copy omitted page 25.
- Evidence status: omission itself is well established; deliberate suppression not established.
- Candidate explanations: filing/scanning omission, archival loss, separation before accession.
- Current status: `LIKELY LOST MATERIAL`
- Primary-source verification/detail expansion: pending.
