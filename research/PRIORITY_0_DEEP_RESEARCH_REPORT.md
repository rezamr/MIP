## Executive finding

The pass reported that public/consumer Gateway material was not the complete record of how Monroe/Hemi-Sync was used inside the U.S. Army CENTER LANE program.

The strongest reported documentary finding is a 26 August 1983 CENTER LANE document describing advanced training that would be individually tailored, would extend beyond the confines of the public program, would last about ten weeks, and would include biomonitoring, custom Hemi-Sync tape development, monitoring methods, controlled environments, and interpretation of information obtained during Hemi-Sync sessions.

This materially weakens any hypothesis that the consumer Gateway tapes/manuals preserve the whole government-relevant implementation.

It does **not** by itself establish secret anomalous technology, a nonlocal information substrate, or present-day classification.

## Major archival anomalies identified

### AA-001 — Missing page 25

The public government copy omitted page 25. Monroe Institute later supplied a complete copy. The omission is real; deliberate suppression is not established.

### AA-002 — Advanced training beyond public Gateway

The reported 26 August 1983 CENTER LANE record explicitly described advanced individualized training beyond the public program.

### AA-003 — Custom government-use Hemi-Sync recordings

The pass reported contract material stating that Monroe would produce custom-made Hemi-Sync tapes for exclusive CENTER LANE personnel, based on demonstrated perceptual abilities, and that the tapes would become government property.

### AA-004 — Missing complete ten-week laboratory record

The plan reportedly covered approximately ten weeks. A March 1984 report identified itself as week four and referred to somatic responses from earlier weeks. A contiguous week-by-week record was not recovered.

### AA-005 — Operational audio engineering finer than consumer manuals

The pass reported a 19–23 March 1984 operational environment containing:

- 100 Hz base with 1.5 Hz binaural beat;
- 200 Hz base with 4 Hz binaural beat.

This was described as an operational multi-frequency Hemi-Sync environment used during two interviews and reportedly as the first operational use of such an environment.

A separate technical source reportedly stated that some Hemi-Sync environments could employ as many as six individual binaural-beat frequencies.

### AA-006 — Biomonitoring in individualized tuning

CENTER LANE reportedly planned use of Monroe non-invasive biomonitoring equipment. The surviving week-four report reportedly compared somatic electrical responses with target-description accuracy.

The exact sensors, filtering, calibration, thresholds and audio-adjustment logic remain unknown.

### AA-007 — Hemi-Sync synthesizer capability

The pass reported contemporaneous distinction between prerecorded tapes and a Hemi-Sync synthesizer, implying multi-component environments rather than a single pure pair. Later patents provide additional engineering clues but cannot automatically be projected backward.

### AA-008 — CENTER LANE mission extended beyond READ

The mission reportedly included expansion beyond remote viewing into psychoenergetic communication and psychokinesis/perturbation. This is evidence of capability sought, not proof that it worked.

### AA-009 — Remote perturbation protocol

A government record reportedly describes attempts to influence random systems mentally while explicitly considering ordinary electronic interference as a confound.

This constitutes a genuine historical outbound experimental branch but not information storage in a hypothesized substrate.

### AA-010 — Later patent capability

A later Monroe patent reportedly includes use of an EEG representation associated with one person's state to induce a related state in another person. This is successor engineering evidence and should be treated as a later capability unless earlier documentation is recovered.

## Most important technical discovery

The reported 1984 operational anchor changes the reconstruction problem.

Current working reported anchor:

- `100 Hz base / 1.5 Hz binaural beat`
- `200 Hz base / 4 Hz binaural beat`

This suggests that historical operational environments may have been multi-component rather than defined by one universal 'Gateway frequency'.

The engineering search space therefore includes:

- multiple simultaneous carrier/beat pairs;
- time sequencing;
- relative amplitude;
- masking/background audio;
- voice timing;
- possible EEG-derived modulation;
- individual calibration;
- environmental configuration;
- physiological feedback.

The project owner's first session used 396 Hz + 4 Hz. The `4 Hz` component therefore has a reported historical overlap pending source verification. The `396 Hz` carrier/base does not match the reported 100/200 Hz operational anchor and remains unsupported historically.

## Outbound / information-direction reconstruction

The report separated historical architectures as follows:

| Architecture | Historical analogue | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| READ | remote viewing / remote perception / precognition | extensive corpus |
| WRITE | psychokinesis / remote perturbation / REG intention | yes, but perturbation rather than persistent information writing |
| TRANSFER | sender/receiver telepathy, dream telepathy, Ganzfeld | yes |
| STORE | psychometry / place-memory literature | weak analogues only; exact deliberate architecture not recovered |
| QUERY | Gateway Five Questions / participant questions in Focus 12 | subjective protocol analogue |
| RESPONSE | imagery/feeling/knowing | phenomenological; external-source attribution unproven |
| HANDSHAKE | blinded query → genuinely unknown challenge → response | exact historical procedure not recovered |

### READ

The strongest branch. Historical remote-viewing/precognition experiments include cases where the participant responded before target selection. This remains READ architecture, not evidence of writing information externally.

### TRANSFER

Sender/receiver telepathy predates Gateway. Later work reportedly suggested that a sender might not always be mechanistically necessary, which is a warning against automatically interpreting apparent A→B performance as A writing information that B later reads.

### WRITE

Historical perturbation/PK and later REG intention experiments are the closest developed outbound analogue, but the architecture is human intention → physical/random apparatus rather than human information → persistent nonlocal store.

### STORE

The exact architecture:

`A deliberately encodes X → no receiver → delay → B retrieves X`

was not recovered as a strong historical laboratory protocol.

Psychometry/object-reading and place-memory literature provide only weak/conceptual analogues and have major methodological limitations.

### QUERY / RESPONSE

Gateway Five Questions provides a historical template for participant question → subjective response. It lacks the critical objective condition in which the answer contains information unavailable to the participant and experimenter at query time.
